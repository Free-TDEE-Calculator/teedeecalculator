# Free TDEE Calculator

Welcome to the official repository for the Free TDEE Calculator. Our web application helps fitness enthusiasts, athletes, and individuals accurately estimate their Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) based on age, gender, height, weight, and daily activity levels.

## About Total Daily Energy Expenditure

Understanding your TDEE is critical for effective weight management, whether your goal is muscle hypertrophy, fat loss, or maintaining your current physique. By calculating your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) using scientifically validated formulas like the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and factoring in your Exercise Activity Thermogenesis (EAT) and Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis (NEAT), our tool provides a reliable baseline for your daily caloric needs.

## Access the Tool

Use the live application: [Free TDEE Calculator](https://freetdeecalculator.app/) to find your personalized maintenance calories, bulking surplus, or cutting deficit targets instantly.

## Features

- Clean, user-friendly interface
- Support for both metric and imperial units
- Instant macronutrient breakdowns for your specific goals
- 9 free calculators: TDEE, BMR, macros, calorie deficit, weight loss, cutting, maintenance, intermittent fasting, and steps-based TDEE
